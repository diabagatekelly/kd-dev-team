# The problem

The first planning document: what is wrong today, in the user's own
account, before anything is decided about how to fix it.

```markdown
# Problem: <short name>

## The problem
The way things are done today and what goes wrong, with the user's own figures.

## Why now
What makes it worth solving instead of leaving alone.

## Constraints
The hard limits the user stated.

## Open questions
What the user said they do not know yet.
```

**Before writing.** Where the user has not spoken to one of the four
sections, ask for it: one question, in plain words, then stop and
wait. "I don't know yet" and "nothing is forcing it" are answers:
record them and do not ask again. Never fill a section from what such
a product usually needs.

**Nothing of the solution.** Ask nothing about how it would be built
or used (the kind of product, its screens, its sign-in, its
technology) and write none of it. An idea the user floats is not a
constraint: leave it out, or keep it as an open question.

**Only what the user said.** No figure, reason, limit, goal or measure
of success of your own, and no further sections. One page, short
bullets.

**Two problems.** If the account holds two problems, each solvable
without the other, say so and ask whether each gets its own document
and which comes first. Do not write one document for both.

**Then stop.** Save it as the document the startup report names for
this stage, say what is written and what is open, and go no further.
