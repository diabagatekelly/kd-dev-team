# The requirements

What a user needs to be able to do in the activity the scope map
marks "now", as a list that is checked off as work is merged. Later
documents point at an item by its name.

```markdown
# Requirements: <the "now" activity>

- [ ] **short-name**: <Who> can <do what>, in one sentence.
- [ ] **another-name**: ...

## Out for now
What the documents raise that is no part of this activity, a line each.

## Limits that apply
Each limit of the scope map: what it forces in this activity, or why it does not apply here.

## Not yet confirmed
Each open question this activity leans on and each question of your own, with what its answer would change. Or "Nothing."

## Outside systems and lasting things
Each by its everyday name, a line each. Or "none".
```

**The items.** Read the problem document and the scope map, then
look at what else the project holds. Where it already has code, read
what that does for this activity:
what the code shows is never asked of the user, and what already
works is said under "Out for now", not listed as an item. Read
nothing else. An item is one thing a user needs to be able to do in the "now"
activity, said so that someone can later answer yes or no to "can
they?". Who is named by their role, never "I", "me" or "you". It ends in something that user wanted: a step on the way is
not an item. It comes from what the user said or it is not there: nothing
such a product usually has, and nothing taken from how it is done
today unless the user asked to keep it. The other rows of the map
give no items;
nor does the reason for doing it now, or a measure of how fast or
how often. What, never how: no technology, screen, design, estimate
or wording of a test. One the user floats in passing is left out of
the whole document.

**Names.** Two to four plain words, lower case, joined by hyphens,
each used once. A name once written is never changed or reused.
Every box is written unchecked: checking is not this stage's.

**Limits and open questions.** A limit is no item and adds none: say
what it forces here. An open question this activity leans on goes under "Not
yet confirmed" and is not settled by an item: an item that would
assume its answer is not written until it is answered. One it does
not touch is left out.

**Your own questions.** Walk every item against each of these:

- **does**: what the person does and is shown, where the item's verb
  does not say (practise, manage, review, handle);
- **who**: who exactly does it, and who may not;
- **who else**: who else must see or do something for it to work;
- **which**: how the thing is told apart; one at a time or several;
- **when**: by when, how often, until when;
- **from where**: who makes or supplies what it starts from;
- **covers**: what a word of the user's takes in and leaves out;
- **put right**: a mistake corrected, a step taken back;
- **from today**: what exists today that must come across.

Where the documents do not say, and the answer would add, remove or
change an item, it is a question. Only what the user alone can answer: never how it is built or how
someone is told, nor what a document or a test can settle, nor a
number asked for its own sake. Every one
that passes, the one whose answer would change the list most first,
under "Not yet confirmed" with the item it bears on; none settled by
an item.

**Outside systems and lasting things.** A system outside the product
that this activity talks to, and a thing the product keeps track of
over time (a loan, a booking), only as the documents name them. What
is used today and would be replaced is neither. The slice and the
approach start from this list.

**Then ask once.** Show the items and ask whether anything is
missing, unwanted or not for now, with every question under "Not yet
confirmed". Make a correction in the document; the other items keep
their names. An answer settles only what it speaks to: a question it
passes over stays, and is asked again.

**Stop there.** Save it as the document the startup report names for
this stage. No slice, no acceptance criteria, and the scope map is
not changed.
