## What it is

The answers (stage 8) leave tests: things that turn on how something
outside behaves, which no page read says. They are in
`NNN_answered.md` under "To test, by harm", 10 to 18 a slice on the
two real plans. This stage settles what can be settled before
anything is designed on a guess, says plainly what stays open, reads
the owner's list, and brings it to them once (decision 53).

- **The session's judgement is one file,** `NNN_open-facts.md` in
  the slice's folder: a block a test, each sorted one of four ways.
- **A script does the rest.** `openfacts/openfacts.py next` lists
  the tests with no block, the gravest first, and the decisions not
  yet read; `run` runs one test's script and keeps what it printed;
  `draw` refuses what does not hold, draws the answers again and
  then `NNN_tested.md`. The stage script counts the stage done while
  that drawing carries the mark of the answers and the open facts,
  and no decision waits for the owner.

| How a test is sorted | When | What becomes of it |
|---|---|---|
| `page` | a page of the thing's documentation would say it | its answer becomes a `look up`; research's second pass looks them all up together (decision 54); found, the answer rests on the new fact and its words |
| `run` | one request to the thing's test environment settles it | a script beside the file, run by the command, its output kept; the result in the output's own words; a fact added to the research document under `## Shown by a test`; the answer changed to rest on it |
| `person` | only a person can: their account, a real card or sign-in, a dashboard, a question to the provider | left open; said to the owner when it is of high harm; settled later by their word, recorded with the date |
| `later` | it shows only once the product is in use | left open |

A test left open says `Either way:` what the slice does so that it
is safe whichever is true; its answer stays a `test` and no
criterion rests on the guess.

**The owner's list is read before it is brought.** Under a last
heading, a line for each decision that waits: theirs (`rule`,
`taste`, `risk`, `scope`), or `chosen` (a technical choice: the
answer becomes a choice), `decided` (a document already decides it:
the answer becomes a fact), `same as A82` (the two are made one).
Then the list is shown once, the gravest first, with each open fact
of high harm; what they decide is recorded by the answers' text, and
a decision they leave is recorded as left. The design does not
start while one waits.

## What the stage must do

1. Every test the answers leave is sorted, the gravest first.
2. What a page says is looked up, not run and not left.
3. A test is run only against a test environment its documentation
   names, with the command, and never against the real thing, even
   when told to.
4. A result is in the words printed; the fact, the answer and its
   criterion follow from it.
5. A key is never in the conversation or in a file; one this machine
   lacks is asked for once, with the command that takes it.
6. What only a person or time can show is left open, with what the
   slice does either way.
7. A result that overturns what was written is said first, and the
   answer and its criterion changed.
8. The owner's list is read: technical choices, things decided and
   the same decision twice are taken off; what is theirs stays.
9. The list is brought once, the gravest first, with options, the
   one recommended and what happens if left; nothing is decided for
   them; what they decide or leave is recorded in their words.
10. Told to skip: once what it costs, then recorded and drawn; the
    list is brought all the same.
11. What the user says a test showed is recorded as theirs.
12. Answers changed after: only what the change brings.

## Three points that are the owner's

1. **A second reader of each claim against its quotation: a model
   reading a model, inside the product.** Not built. After stage 8,
   about one answer in fifteen that speaks of a thing committed to
   says what nothing read says, and five to nine in thirty quote the
   wrong line of a fact that does say it. A script cannot read
   whether words say a thing. Proposed: a fresh reader, given only
   each such answer's `Says` and the words it quotes, says of each
   whether the words say it, say less, or are silent; an answer it
   flags goes back to be looked up or is cut back to its words. It
   grades no stage and decides nothing: its only power is to send an
   answer back. Its test: the 120 answers two sessions already read
   (four kept runs), where eight claims are known to rest on nothing.
   Bar: it flags at least six of the eight, and at most one in ten
   of those the read found supported. A real slice has 40 to 80 such
   answers: about 30 cents a slice on Sonnet by estimate. The other
   way: no reader, and about four such claims a slice reach the
   design.
2. **The stage sends to a provider's test environment, with a test
   key the user gives.** Decision 52 has a planning VM read any
   website and send to none. A test that is run must send. As built:
   the user allows the one test address for the VM, with the command
   research already gives for a site, and types the key at a hidden
   prompt in a terminal of the VM; refused either, the test is left
   open. For the two real plans this means the provider's own
   Sandbox keys in the evaluation VM and its sandbox address
   allowed: both are the owner's to give. Without them the real runs
   sort, look up and read the list, and run nothing.
3. **The cost below**, and the pilot first.

  - on the two real plans, three runs each: every test sorted;
