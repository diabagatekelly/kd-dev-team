# Scope map: Practising the mutashabihat in the Qur'an

| # | What the user does | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep track of where we slip | The places where a teacher or listener stops us or a test shows a slip, and the pairs we keep from them | next |
| 2 | Compare similar verses | Reading them side by side | later |
| 3 | Practise telling apart from memory the ones we get wrong | Doing this on our own, at the moment we reach one of these verses | now |

## Holds for every activity
- The text of the Qur'an must be shown exactly, with nothing changed or approximated. This is not negotiable.
