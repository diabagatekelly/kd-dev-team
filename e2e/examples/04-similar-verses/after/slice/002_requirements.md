# Requirements: practise telling similar verses apart from memory

- [ ] **practise-own-slips**: A learner can practise, on their own between tests, the similar verses they get wrong.
- [ ] **tell-apart-from-memory**: A learner can practise, on their own between tests, telling similar verses apart from memory at the moment of reaching one.

## Out for now

- Others who memorise using it: we are first. If it worked, we would want them to use it.

## Limits that apply

- Exact text of the Qur'an: any of it shown to the learner in this activity is shown exactly, with nothing changed or approximated. This is not negotiable.

## Not yet confirmed

Questions of our own, the one that would change the list most first:

1. **Which ones are wrong** (practise-own-slips): how is it settled that the learner gets a verse wrong: from what a teacher or listener caught, from what happens in the learner's own practice, from the learner's own say-so, or more than one of these? The answer could add items for recording or changing which ones these are.
2. **What practising is** (tell-apart-from-memory): at the moment of reaching one, what is the learner given, what do they do, and what are they shown afterwards? Said so far: only a vague picture, from being tested, of being given the start of a verse and carrying on, or being shown two wordings and asked which comes where. We don't know which, and suspect there are better ways we haven't met. Still open. The answer could change this item or split it.
3. **Right or wrong** (both): who or what decides whether the learner told them apart correctly? The answer could add another person or an item.
4. **What is covered** (both): similar verses are verses, or parts of verses, in different places that are worded almost the same, with a word or an ending that differs; reciting from memory, we slide from one into the other without noticing. The ones we get wrong matter most. Which pairs a learner confuses differs from one person to another and depends on what they have memorised and how. Not decided: should practice also cover every similar verse there is? Not yet answered: are they only pairs, or also larger groups? The answer would change what both items take in, and could add an item.
5. **A teacher or anyone else** (both): the learner is us first. Does a teacher or anyone else need to see or do anything? The answer could add people and items.
6. **What comes across** (practise-own-slips): must the places marked in our own copy and the pairs in our notebook be brought in? The answer could add an item.
7. **Putting right** (practise-own-slips): if a verse is wrongly counted as one the learner gets wrong, does the learner need to correct that? The answer could add an item.
8. **No longer wrong** (practise-own-slips): does a verse ever stop counting as one the learner gets wrong, and if so, when? The answer could add or change an item.
9. **Which text** (both): which reading (riwayah) and which script or printing must the text match? The answer would change what "exactly" means for both items.

Open questions from the scope map:

- Does a list of the similar verses exist in a form an app could use? We have never looked. The answer would change where the verses practised come from, and could add an item for supplying them.
- Is there a source for the text of the Qur'an itself that an app could use? We have never looked. Both items lean on it wherever text is shown, which must be exact.
- What may it cost? The answer could limit how either item can be met.
- Who would look after it? The answer could limit how either item can be met, or add a person.
