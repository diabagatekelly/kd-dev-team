## Where to start
- Neither slice can be started yet.
- Slice 1 waits on two questions under "Not yet confirmed" in the requirements: what you do and are shown when you practise (question 1), and whether a source for the exact text exists (question 6). Without the text nothing can be shown, and the text must be exact.
- Slice 2 waits on the same text source (question 6), and on which pairs count as ones you get wrong and where they come from (question 2), which also leans on whether a usable list of the similar verses exists.
- Recommended first: slice 1. It is the practice itself, it shows a person the most for the least, and slice 2 builds on it.
