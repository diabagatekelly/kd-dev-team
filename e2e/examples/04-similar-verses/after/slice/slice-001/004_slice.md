# Slice 1: Tell apart from memory one pair of similar verses the learner gets wrong

## Thin path

A learner, on their own between tests, practises one pair of similar verses they get wrong, and has to tell the two apart from memory. What they are given, what they do and what they are shown afterwards wait on our decision.

## Criteria

These two follow from the requirements as they stand. They are not the whole slice: criteria that depend on an unanswered question are not written yet.

- **practise-own-slips / 1**: Given one pair of similar verses counted as one the learner gets wrong, when the learner starts practising on their own, then that pair is what they are given to practise. *Waits on our decision: how a pair comes to be counted as wrong.*
- **tell-apart-from-memory / 1**: Given a pair of similar verses in practice, with any text of the Qur'an shown exactly, when the learner reaches the point where the two can be confused, then they are called on to tell them apart before being shown wording that gives the answer away. *Waits on our decision: what the learner is given and what they do, and which text "exactly" is measured against.*

Not written yet, waiting on our decision:

- What the learner is shown after trying, and who or what decides whether they were right.
- What happens when no pair is counted as one the learner gets wrong.

## Needs from outside

- A source for the text of the Qur'an, shown exactly. Not known whether one exists that an app could use.
- Knowing which verses are similar to which. Not known whether a list exists in a form an app could use.

## Left for later

- The rest of the pairs the learner gets wrong: slice 2. Usable without it: the learner can practise the one pair.
- Similar verses in groups larger than two, if they are covered: slice 2. Usable without it: the learner can practise a pair.

## Size

Criteria: 2.
