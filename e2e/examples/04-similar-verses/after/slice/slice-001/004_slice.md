# Slice 1: Practise telling the verses of one pair apart from memory

## Thin path
A learner who memorises takes one pair of similar verses, tries from memory to tell the two apart, and is then shown how they did and the exact text of both verses.

## Criteria
- **practise-from-memory / 1**: Given a pair of similar verses is offered for practice, when the learner starts, then they are asked to tell the two verses apart without the text of the pair in front of them.
- **practise-from-memory / 2**: Given the learner has answered from memory, when they look at the result, then they are shown whether the answer was right and the text of both verses exactly as the source gives it, with nothing changed or approximated.

## Needs from outside
- A source for the exact text of the Qur'an (not yet found).
- At least one pair of similar verses to practise (where it comes from is not yet known).
