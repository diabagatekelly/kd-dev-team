# Scope map: Practising the mutashabihat of the Qur'an

| # | What the user does | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Practise telling similar verses apart from memory | Reaching one from memory and telling it apart from its pair, on our own between tests; the thing that never gets practised today | now |
| 2 | Read similar verses side by side | Seeing the two verses together, which helps for a few days | next |
| 3 | Keep track of the pairs we slip on | What we now do with marks in our copy and a notebook; knowing where we slip, which the tests showed us | later |

## Holds for every activity
- The text of the Qur'an must be shown exactly, with nothing changed or approximated. This is not negotiable.

## Open
- Does a list of the similar verses exist in a form an app could use? We have never looked.
- Is there a source for the text of the Qur'an itself? We have never looked.
- What may it cost?
- Who would look after it?
- Should practising pick out the pairs we get wrong (which would bring "keep track of the pairs we slip on" closer to the first row), or should every similar verse be practised alike? We are not ready to decide.
