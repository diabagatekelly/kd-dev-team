# Requirements: Practise telling similar verses apart from memory

- [x] **tell-apart-from-memory**: A learner can practise telling a similar verse apart from its pair from memory, at the moment they reach one.
- [x] **practise-on-own**: A learner can do that practice on their own, between tests by a teacher.
- [ ] **practise-weak-pairs**: A learner can practise the pairs they get wrong, which matter most; which pairs those are differs from one learner to another, depending on what they have memorised and how.

## Out for now
- Reading similar verses side by side (row 2 of the scope map).
- Keeping track of the pairs we slip on (row 3 of the scope map).
- What it may cost and who would look after it.
- Others who memorise using it: we want that if it works, but us first.

## Limits that apply
- The text of the Qur'an must be shown exactly, with nothing changed or approximated: every verse shown while practising is its exact text, never altered or approximated.
