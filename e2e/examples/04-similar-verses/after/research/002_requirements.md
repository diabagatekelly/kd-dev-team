# Requirements: Practise telling similar verses apart from memory

- [ ] **tell-apart**: A student can practise, on their own between tests, telling a verse, or part of a verse, apart from the ones that resemble it, from memory.
- [ ] **wrong-first**: A student can practise the similar verses they get wrong before the others.

## Out for now
- Looking verses up the way the printed book does: it is for looking things up, not for practising.

## Limits that apply
- The text of the Qur'an must be shown exactly, with nothing changed or approximated. In this activity that forces every verse the student is shown, including each look-alike, to be the exact text, never shortened, reworded or approximated.

## Not yet confirmed
- **What the student does and is shown (tell-apart):** we only have a vague picture, from being tested: being given the start of a verse and carrying on, or being shown two wordings and asked which comes where. We don't know which, and suspect there are better ways we haven't met. Left open: it decides what the item means.
- **How "the ones I get wrong" are known (wrong-first):** which pairs we confuse is personal: it depends on what we have memorised and how, so it is not the same as for someone else. Not yet said: whether we tell the app which ones, or it learns that from how we do in practice, and whether the pairs already marked in our copy and notebook must come across. The answer decides what the item needs to start from.
- **What counts as similar verses (tell-apart):** verses, or parts of verses, in different places, worded almost the same, with a word or an ending that differs. Not known yet: whether it must also cover every similar verse there is, and whether groups of three or more count as well as pairs. The answer changes which verses can be practised.
- **Whether a list of the similar verses exists in a form an app could use (open since the problem):** we have never looked. If none does, tell-apart has nothing to start from until one is found or made.
- **Whether there is a source for the text (open since the problem):** we have never looked. The exact-text limit cannot be met without one.
- **What it may cost, and who would look after it (open since the problem):** not known yet. The answers could change how much of the list and the text can be covered.

## Outside systems and lasting things
- The text of the Qur'an: an outside source, not yet known.
- The list of similar verses: an outside source, not yet known.
- The verses the student gets wrong: kept over time, so that they can be practised first.
