# Slice 1: Practise telling a verse apart from one that resembles it, from memory, over a small set of pairs

## Thin path
A student is shown a place in the text and the two similar wordings that could go there, and picks the one that belongs. They are then told whether they were right and shown the exact text of both wordings and where each belongs. This takes the form you named of being shown two wordings and asked which comes where; the other form you named (being given the start of a verse and carrying on) is not in this slice. Taking this form is mine, not yours: please confirm or change it.

## Criteria
- **tell-apart / 1**: Given a small set of similar pairs is available, when the student starts to practise, then they are shown a place in the text and the two similar wordings that resemble each other, and are asked which belongs there.
- **tell-apart / 2**: Given a place and its two wordings are shown, when the student picks the wording that belongs there, then they are told it is right and are shown the exact text of both wordings and the place each belongs to, with nothing changed, shortened or approximated.
- **tell-apart / 3**: Given a place and its two wordings are shown, when the student picks the wording that does not belong there, then they are told it is wrong and are shown which one belongs, with the exact text of both wordings and the place each belongs to, with nothing changed, shortened or approximated.
- **tell-apart / 4**: Given the set of pairs is empty, when the student starts to practise, then they are told there is nothing to practise yet and are shown no place and no wordings.
