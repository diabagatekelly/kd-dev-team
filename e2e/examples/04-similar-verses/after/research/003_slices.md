# Slices: Practise telling similar verses apart from memory

| # | Slice | Items | Builds on | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Practise telling a verse apart from one that resembles it, from memory, over a small set of pairs | tell-apart | nothing | chosen |
| 2 | Practise the pairs I get wrong before the others | wrong-first | 1 | open |
| 3 | Practise across every similar verse there is, including groups of three or more | tell-apart | 1 | open |

## Where to start
- Slice 1 is the one to start with: it is the practice you have never had, over the smallest set of pairs, and the other two build on it. It cannot be started yet. It leans on three questions that are still open: what you do and are shown when practising, whether a usable list of similar verses exists, and whether there is a source for the text. The exact-text limit cannot be met without that source.
- Slice 2 waits for slice 1, and for the question of how the ones you get wrong are known (you tell the app, or it learns from your practice, and whether the pairs in your copy and notebook come across).
- Slice 3 waits for slice 1, and for the question of whether it must cover every similar verse there is and whether groups of three or more count. It cannot be started.
- What the cost may be and who would look after it touch all three.
