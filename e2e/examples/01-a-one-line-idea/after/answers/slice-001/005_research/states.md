## For the owner's list
- `brought-across`: no way out. The list keeper has the list in the order it was brought across. Changing it, or putting right a wrong place, is left for later (slice-001/004_slice.md lines 17 and 18).
- `behind-notebook`: no way out. The list brought across no longer matches the notebook if the change affects what it holds; the notebook can still be kept up to date and checked against.
