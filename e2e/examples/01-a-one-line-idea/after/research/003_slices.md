# Slices: Find out where I am on the list

| # | Slice | Items | Builds on | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | See my own place on the waiting list | see-own-place, others-stay-private | nothing | chosen |

## Where to start
- Slice 1 can be started now. It is the ordinary path: a person waiting is shown their own place and nothing about anyone else, with the list starting as the notebook's order. It shows a person the most for the least, and slice 2 builds on it. I recommend it first.
- Slice 2 cannot be started: it leans on the open question of how one person is told apart from another on the list. It waits until that is answered.
- The other open question, what "where they stand" covers beyond the place in the order, is not cut into a slice. If the answer adds something, the item changes at the requirements stage first.
