# Slice 1: Carry the notebook's list over in the same order

## Thin path
The person who keeps the list carries everyone in the paper notebook over to the waiting list, and is shown them in the same order as in the notebook.

## Criteria
The people in this example are invented.

- **carry-over-list / 1**: Given an empty waiting list, and a paper notebook that lists Person A, Person B and Person C in that order, when the person who keeps the list carries the notebook over, then the waiting list they are shown holds those three and no one else, each once, in the order Person A, Person B, Person C.

## Needs from outside
none

## Left for later
- A person on the waiting list seeing their own place: slice 2. Usable without it: the person who keeps the list can have the notebook's list carried over and check its order, and people can still phone to ask where they are, as they do today.
- Seeing a place after the list has changed: slice 3. Usable without it: the list can be carried over as the notebook stands, and the person who keeps the list can still record later changes in the notebook. The carried-over list will not show those changes.

## Size
Criteria: 1.
