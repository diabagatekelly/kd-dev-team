## Where to start
- Slice 1 can be started now. It is the recommended start: it leans on no open question, and the other slices build on it.
- Slice 2 waits for slice 1, because a person's place comes from the list. It covers only the person's own place in the order, with no other people's names shown to everyone.
- Slice 3 cannot be started until the questions under "Not yet confirmed" in the requirements are answered: what changes keep the list up to date, who else may see where a person stands, what needs to be possible when a place is wrong, what else "where they stand" takes in, and what moves a person down the list or off it. The answers may change this set.
