# Problem: allotment waiting list and plot offers

## The problem
- We have 84 plots and about 40 people waiting for one.
- The waiting list is a paper notebook kept at one of our homes, and people phone to ask where they are on it. Telling them takes about an evening a week on the phone.
- When a plot comes free, the committee is meant to offer it to whoever is first. Twice last year it went to someone's friend, and there was a row at the annual meeting.

## Why now
- The council owns the land, and it has said the lease renewal in March depends on us showing that plots are given out fairly.

## Constraints
- We can spend 10 pounds a month at most.
- The committee are five volunteers, none of us technical.
- Many members are elderly and several have no smartphone.

## Open questions
- Should collecting the plot rent be part of this? The treasurer collects it by cheque once a year and hasn't said.
