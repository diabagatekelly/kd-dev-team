# Slice 1: The keeper puts the notebook's waiting list in, in the order it is in now

## Thin path
The keeper enters the people on the waiting list, one after another in the order the notebook has them, and is shown the list in that same order.

## Criteria
- **keep-the-list / 1**: Given a waiting list with nobody on it, when the keeper enters three people one after another, say Ana, then Ben, then Cai (invented), then the keeper is shown the list with Ana first, Ben second and Cai third.
- **keep-the-list / 2**: Given the keeper has entered those three people and left, when the keeper comes back later and looks at the list, then Ana, Ben and Cai are still there in the same order.

## Needs from outside
none

## Left for later
- Members finding out their own place: slice 2. Usable without it: members phone the keeper as they do now, and the keeper can read their place from the list shown.
- Keeping the list up to date as people join, leave or are given a plot, including what makes two entries the same person: slice 3. Usable without it: the keeper goes on updating the notebook as now, and takes care not to enter anyone twice.

Criteria: 2.
