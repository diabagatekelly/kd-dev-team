## For the owner's list
- `part-carried-over`: no way out. The documents do not say what the person who keeps the list is left with if carrying over is not finished.
- `behind-notebook`: no way out. The carried-over list does not show later changes recorded in the notebook.
- `wrong`: no way out. A wrong or disputed place has no stated way to be corrected.
- `unavailable`: no way out. The documents do not say what anyone is left with if the list stops working.
- `carry-over-refused`: no way out. The documents do not say what the person who keeps the list is left with if carrying over is always refused.
- `ended`: no way out. The documents do not say what is left of the list, or whether it can be started again.
- `carried-place`: no way out. The place stays as carried over; no change to it is stated.
- `newly-joined`: no way out. The documents do not say what happens to the place of someone who joins from now on.
- `moved`: no way out. The documents do not say whether places move, or what follows if one does.
- `taken-off`: no way out. The documents do not say whether someone taken off can come back.
