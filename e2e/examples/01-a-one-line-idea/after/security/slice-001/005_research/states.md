## The waiting list

| State | Entered by | What the person can do | Way out | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `not-carried-across` | Where it starts: nothing has been carried across | The list keeper can have it carried across | The list keeper has it carried across -> `carried-across` | "Given nothing has been carried across" (slice-001/004_slice.md, line 7) |
| `being-carried-across` | not said in the documents | not said in the documents | none | not said in the documents |

| `out-of-date` | The list not being changed or corrected once it is across; which events call for a change is not said in the documents | People can see places that are out of date, once they can see places | none | "the places seen can go out of date or stay wrong" (003_slices.md, line 15) |
| `wrong` | not said in the documents | People can see places that stay wrong, once they can see places | none | "the places seen can go out of date or stay wrong" (003_slices.md, line 15) |
| `ended` | not said in the documents, including what is left afterwards | not said in the documents | none | not said in the documents |

## For the owner's list
- `being-carried-across`: no way out. The documents do not say what the list keeper is left with while it is only partly across.
- `carry-across-failed`: no way out. The documents do not say what the list keeper is left with.
- `carried-across`: no way out. The list keeper has the list as carried across; no slice includes changing it.
- `out-of-date`: no way out. The places on the list can stay out of date.
- `wrong`: no way out. The places on the list can stay wrong.
- `ended`: no way out. The documents do not say what is left or whether the list can be started again.
