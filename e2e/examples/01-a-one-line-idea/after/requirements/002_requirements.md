# Requirements: A member finds out where they are on the list

- [ ] **see-own-place**: A person on the waiting list can find out where they are on the list, without the committee telling them on the phone.
- [ ] **names-kept-private**: A person on the waiting list sees only their own place, and other people's names are not shown to everyone.
- [x] **list-comes-across**: The person who keeps the list can have the notebook's list, in exactly its present order, as the list people find their place on.
- [ ] **keep-list-current**: The person who keeps the list can add people to it and take people off it, so that places stay right.

## Out for now
- Offering a free plot to whoever is first on the list (the next activity on the scope map).

## Limits that apply
- £10 a month at most: whatever is used for this activity has to fit within it.
- Five non-technical volunteers on the committee: the committee has to be able to keep the list going without technical help.
- Many members are elderly and several have no smartphone: a person with no smartphone has to be able to find out their place as well. This adds no separate item.

## Not yet confirmed
- **which**: Is there one waiting list, or several (for example by site or kind of plot)? Several would mean a person's place has to say which list it is on.
- **covers**: Does "a person on the waiting list" take in everyone on it, including people who are not members yet? Either way it could change who can find their place.

## Outside systems and lasting things
- The waiting list, and each person's place on it (a paper notebook today, about 40 people waiting for 84 plots).
