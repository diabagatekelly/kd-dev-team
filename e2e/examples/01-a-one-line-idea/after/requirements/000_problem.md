# Problem: the plot waiting list

## The problem
- We have 84 plots and about 40 people waiting for one.
- The waiting list is a paper notebook that I keep at home, and people phone me to ask where they are on it. I spend about an evening a week on the phone telling people where they are on the list.
- When a plot comes free, the committee is meant to offer it to whoever is first. Twice last year it went to someone's friend instead, and there was a row at the annual meeting.

## Why now
- The council owns the land, and it has said the lease renewal in March depends on us showing that plots are given out fairly.

## Constraints
- We can spend 10 pounds a month at most.
- The committee is five volunteers, none of us technical.
- Many members are elderly, and several have no smartphone.
