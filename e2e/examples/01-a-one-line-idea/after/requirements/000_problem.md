# Problem: Allotment society plot list

## The problem
- When a plot comes free, the committee is meant to offer it to whoever is first on the list. Twice last year it went to someone's friend, and there was a row at the annual meeting.
- I spend about an evening a week on the phone telling people where they are on the list.

## Why now
- The council owns the land, and it has said the lease renewal in March depends on us showing that plots are given out fairly.

## Constraints
- We can spend £10 a month at most.
- The committee are five volunteers, none of us technical.
- Many members are elderly and several have no smartphone.

## Open questions
- I don't know whether collecting the plot rent should be part of this. The treasurer collects it by cheque once a year and hasn't said.
