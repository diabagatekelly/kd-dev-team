# Problem: monthly client invoicing

## The problem
We run a dog-walking business with four walkers and about sixty regular clients.

- Every month, we type up an invoice for each client by hand from the walkers' paper sheets, then send each client a payment link through Tallypay.
- This takes two full days.
- We get two or three invoices wrong every month.
- About one client in ten pays late because nobody chases them.
- We have lost a walker's sheet twice this year and had to guess.

## Why now
- In the summer we take on holiday cover and it roughly doubles, so we would like to sort this out before then.

## Constraints
- We already use Tallypay as our card service, and we are staying with it.

## Open questions
- Whether the walkers should be paid through this too. This is not decided.
