## Chosen without asking; the owner can overturn any of these

- A22: Asking for an invoice only reads clients, walks and prices. Asking again, or twice at once, shows the same invoice from the same walks and prices and makes no second charge. The request cannot change or remove a walk or price, whatever is sent with it. While it is being made the owner sees that it is. Because: a request that only reads is safe to repeat and gives nothing to tamper through
- A23: An invoice is made from the walks and prices as they stood together at one moment, and shows when that was. If a walk or price of it changes afterwards, the shown invoice is marked out of date the next time it is looked at, including after stepping back or in another window, and is not presented as current. A client and month with no walks say so rather than showing an empty or old invoice. Because: an old invoice taken for current is how a wrong charge goes out
- A24: Where a client has no walks put in for the month asked for, the owner is told so. A walk not yet put in, such as one still on a sheet, cannot be on an invoice; the invoice lists every walk it is made from so the owner can compare it with the sheets. Because: the product knows only what has been put into it
- A25: A month's walks are invoiced the same way whether the client is new, has changed details or has since left: the walks stay with the same client. Nothing about a client changes by itself or because an invoice is got. Because: the walks happened and the item charges the month's walks (requirements line 3)
- A26: Time passing, reopening or restarting changes no walk and no price entry. A walk with no list price stays shown as unpriced until its length has a price. Nothing is removed or priced by itself. Because: a change nobody made would alter a charge unnoticed
- A27: The owner's own list is the one that stands. The product shows when its copy of the prices was last brought in, shows the prices it holds so the owner can compare them, and keeps no change of its own in this slice. A list is taken in whole or not at all; a new one that is refused or cut off leaves the earlier one in use. A length given twice with different prices is refused. Two attempts to change the list at once leave one whole list. Because: a part-replaced or doubled list charges wrong amounts
- A28: Prices can be changed only by the signed-in owner. Each time prices change the product records when and what changed, and the owner can see the prices in use with that time. A price altered outside the product's own ways is treated as damage: no invoice is made from it. Earlier lists stay recoverable from the record. Because: a wrong price is charged to every walk of that length

A save that fails, for lack of space or any storage error, says the walk was not saved, leaves earlier walks as they were, and is not reported saved on a mere retry: what is stored is read back first. The owner sees which walks are saved after returning. Because: a walk wrongly believed saved goes uncharged

A part-restored or damaged set is detected and refused. Because: parts restored apart would disagree; clearable storage loses walks silently

A true first run is told apart from data that cannot be opened. An unfinished save is discarded whole and the owner told. Data that can be read but not changed is shown as read only, with nothing reported saved. A later version's data that this version does not understand is refused, not rewritten. Because: starting fresh over damaged data destroys what could be recovered
