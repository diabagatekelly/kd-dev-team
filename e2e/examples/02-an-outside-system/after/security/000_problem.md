# Problem: monthly client invoicing

## The problem
We run a dog-walking business with four walkers and about sixty regular clients. Every month we sit down with the walkers' paper sheets, type up an invoice for each client by hand, then send each client a payment link through Tallypay, the card service we use.

- Invoicing by hand takes two full days a month.
- We get two or three invoices wrong every month.
- About one client in ten pays late, because nobody chases them.
- We have lost a walker's sheet twice this year, and had to guess.

## Why now
- We want to sort this out before the summer, when we take on holiday cover and it roughly doubles.

## Constraints
- We already use Tallypay as our card service, and we are staying with it.

## Open questions
- Should the walkers be paid through this too? Not decided.
