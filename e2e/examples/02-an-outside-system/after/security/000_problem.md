# Problem: monthly invoicing and chasing payment

## The problem
- We run a dog-walking business with four walkers and about sixty regular clients.
- Every month I sit down with the walkers' paper sheets, type up an invoice for each client by hand, and send each one a payment link through Tallypay. It takes me two full days.
- I get two or three invoices wrong every month.
- About one client in ten pays late because nobody chases them.
- I've lost a walker's sheet twice this year (2026) and had to guess.

## Why now
- We want this sorted before the summer, when we take on holiday cover and it roughly doubles.

## Constraints
- Tallypay stays. It is the card service we already use, and we have a contract with it until next year (2027).
- The walkers will only use their phones.
- Clients' addresses and door codes must be seen only by me and the walker for that client.

## Open questions
- Whether the walkers should be paid through this too. I haven't decided.
- Whether this could be done with a WhatsApp bot or something.
