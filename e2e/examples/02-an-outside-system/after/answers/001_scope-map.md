# Scope map: monthly client invoicing

| # | What the user does | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record the walks done | Walkers note their walks, which the month's invoices are made from | next |
| 2 | Invoice clients for the month | Each client gets an invoice for the month and a payment link through Tallypay | now |
| 3 | Chase late payments | Clients who have not paid on time are followed up | later |

## Holds for every activity
- We already use Tallypay as our card service, and we are staying with it.
