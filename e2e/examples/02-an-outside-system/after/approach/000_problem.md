# Problem: Monthly client invoicing

## The problem
- We run a dog-walking business with four walkers and about sixty regular clients.
- Every month I sit down with the walkers' paper sheets and type up an invoice for each client by hand, then send each client a payment link through Tallypay. This takes me two full days.
- Because it is typed by hand, I get two or three invoices wrong every month.
- About one client in ten pays late, because nobody chases them.
- I have lost a walker's sheet twice this year and had to guess.

## Why now
- Over the summer we take on holiday cover, which roughly doubles the work. I want this sorted before then.

## Constraints
- We are staying with Tallypay, the card service we already use.

## Open questions
- Whether a WhatsApp bot could do it (my idea; not decided).
- Whether the invoicing (time, mistakes, lost sheets) and the late payments should be two separate problems. I don't know yet.
