# Problem: Monthly client invoicing

## The problem
- We run a dog-walking business with four walkers and about sixty regular clients.
- Every month I sit down with the walkers' paper sheets and type up an invoice for each client by hand, then send each client a payment link through Tallypay. This takes me two full days.
- Because it is typed by hand, I get two or three invoices wrong every month.
- About one client in ten pays late, because nobody chases them.
- Twice this year I have lost a walker's sheet and had to guess.

## Why now
- We take on holiday cover in the summer, and that roughly doubles the work. We want this sorted before then.

## Constraints
- Tallypay stays: it is the card service we already use, and we have a contract with it until next year.
- The walkers will only use their phones.
- Clients' addresses and door codes must be seen only by me and the walker for that client.

## Open questions
- Whether the walkers should be paid through this too. I haven't decided.
- Whether a WhatsApp bot is the way to do it. I asked, and it is not decided.
