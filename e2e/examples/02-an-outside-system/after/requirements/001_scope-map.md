# Scope map: Monthly client invoicing

| # | What the user does | What it covers | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Walkers record their walks | Each walker's walks, kept safe and in one place, in place of the paper sheets | now |
| 2 | Prepare each client's monthly invoice | The owner gets a correct invoice for each of the sixty or so clients without two days of typing | next |
| 3 | Send each client a payment link | Each client gets their invoice with a Tallypay payment link | next |
| 4 | Chase clients who pay late | Clients who have not paid are followed up | later |

## Holds for every activity
- We stay with Tallypay: there is a contract with them until next year.
- The walkers will only use their phones.
- Clients' addresses and door codes must be seen only by the owner and the walker for that client.

## Open
- Whether the walkers should be paid through this too. Not decided.
