# Problem: Monthly client invoicing

## The problem
- We are a dog-walking business with four walkers and about sixty regular clients.
- Every month I sit down with the walkers' paper sheets and type up an invoice for each client by hand, then send each one a payment link through Tallypay, our card service.
- It takes me two full days each month.
- I get two or three invoices wrong every month.
- About one client in ten pays late, because nobody chases them.
- I have lost a walker's sheet twice this year and had to guess.

## Why now
- Holiday cover this summer roughly doubles the work, and we want this sorted before then.

## Constraints
- We stay with Tallypay: we have a contract with them until next year.
- The walkers will only use their phones.
- Clients' addresses and door codes must be seen only by me and the walker for that client.

## Open questions
- Whether the walkers should be paid through this too. We have not decided.
