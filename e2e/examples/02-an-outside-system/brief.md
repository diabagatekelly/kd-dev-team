# Monthly invoices for a dog-walking business

A full account in the first message, an outside system the work leans on, and a tool floated in passing.

## Opening
I run a dog-walking business, four walkers and about sixty regular clients. Every month I sit down with the walkers' paper sheets and type up an invoice for each client by hand, then send each one a payment link through Tallypay, which is the card service we already use and are staying with. It takes me two full days, I get two or three invoices wrong every month, and about one client in ten pays late because nobody chases them. I've lost a walker's sheet twice this year and had to guess. I'd like to sort this out before the summer, when we take on holiday cover and it roughly doubles. Could we do it with a WhatsApp bot or something?

docs/planning

## Who you are
The owner of the business. Talkative, organised, answers fully, likes to get on with things.
Asked where the planning documents should go, say: put them in docs/planning.

## What you know
- A regular client has a standing arrangement: the same walks every week (say Monday, Wednesday,
  Friday), until they change or stop it. One-off extra walks happen too.
- A walk is 30 or 60 minutes, priced differently. A second dog from the same home costs half.
- Walkers write each walk on a paper sheet and hand the sheets in at the end of the month.
- A walk is sometimes cancelled. Cancelled with a day's notice it is not charged; later than that it is.
- Tallypay takes the card payment and pays out to the business account. You make each payment
  link by hand on its website. You do not know what else Tallypay can do.
- Limits: Tallypay stays (you have a contract until next year). The walkers will only use their
  phones. Clients' addresses and door codes must be seen only by you and the walker for that client.
- Nobody but you sends invoices.

## What you do not know
- Whether Tallypay can send reminders or make links without the website.
- Whether walkers should be paid through this too. You have not decided: say it is open.
- How many invoices are disputed.

## How you behave

- When shown the map, agree with it unless paying the walkers appears as an activity: if it does,
  say that is not decided yet.
- When shown the requirements and asked questions, answer every one as well as the brief allows,
  and say plainly "I don't know" to what it does not cover.
- If asked to choose between ways of building it, say that is for the builder to decide.
