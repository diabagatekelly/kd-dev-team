# Slices: A singer tells us they will miss a rehearsal

| # | Slice | Items | Builds on | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A section leader records that a singer who phoned will miss a rehearsal | record-phoned-absence | nothing | chosen |

## Where to start
- Slice 1 can be started now. Slice 2 waits for slice 1, because the way an absence is kept in the register is built with slice 1.
- None of the open questions stops either slice: they concern how far ahead, several rehearsals at once, taking an absence back, late or early, a reason, and who else may tell us. Each would change or add to a slice later, not the ordinary path of these two.
- I recommend starting with slice 1. It happens at the hall's computer, where the register already is and section leaders already work, so it shows the most for the least: from that moment an absence is known before the night. The singers' own way, which has no password, comes second.
