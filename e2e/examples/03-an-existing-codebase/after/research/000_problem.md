# Problem: Knowing in advance who will miss a rehearsal

## The problem
- We are about 45 singers in four voice parts and we rehearse every Thursday.
- A singer who will miss a rehearsal texts me, or tells a friend, or says nothing.
- So I find out on the night that, for example, the tenors are three short, and by then I can't rearrange the programme.
- A singer who misses three rehearsals in a row without a word is asked whether they're still coming. The secretary works that out by hand today.

## Why now
- The spring concert is in ten weeks, and two of the pieces can't be sung with fewer than four tenors.

## Constraints
- Singers mustn't need a password: several are in their eighties.
- The register is on a small computer at the church hall that the section leaders use.
