# Problem: knowing who will miss a rehearsal

## The problem
- We're about 45 singers in four voice parts, and we rehearse every Thursday.
- A singer who'll miss a rehearsal texts me, or tells a friend, or says nothing.
- I find out on the night that, for example, the tenors are three short, and by then I can't rearrange the programme.
- A singer who misses three rehearsals in a row without a word is asked whether they're still coming. The secretary works that out by hand today.

## Why now
- The spring concert is ten weeks away (as of 25 November 2026), and two of the pieces can't be sung with fewer than four tenors.

## Constraints
- Singers mustn't need a password, because several are in their eighties.
- The register is on a small computer at the church hall that the section leaders use.

## Open questions
- Can singers reach the hall's computer from home? I think not, but I'm not sure.
