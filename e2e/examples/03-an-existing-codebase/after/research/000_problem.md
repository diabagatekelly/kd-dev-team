# Problem: hearing about absences too late

## The problem

- We are about 45 singers in four voice parts, and we rehearse every Thursday.
- A singer who will miss a rehearsal texts us, tells a friend, or says nothing.
- We find out on the night that, for example, the tenors are three short, and by then we can't rearrange the programme.
- A singer who misses three rehearsals in a row without a word is asked whether they're still coming. The secretary works that out by hand.

## Why now

- The spring concert is ten weeks away (as of 2026-11-25), and two of the pieces can't be sung with fewer than four tenors.

## Constraints

- Singers mustn't need a password, because several are in their eighties.
- The register is on a small computer at the church hall, which the section leaders use.

## Open questions

- Can singers reach the hall's computer from home? We think not, but we're not sure.
