# Requirements: A singer tells us they will miss a rehearsal

- [ ] **tell-absence**: A singer can tell us, by the Tuesday evening before a Thursday rehearsal, that they will miss it.
- [ ] **record-phoned-absence**: A section leader can record, for a singer who phones them, that the singer will miss a rehearsal.

## Out for now
- Seeing who will be away, by voice part, before the night (row 2 of the scope map; we have said we need it by voice part).
- The check for singers who have missed three rehearsals in a row without a word (set aside for its own document).
- Recording who came to a rehearsal and reporting it by voice part: the register already does this, so it is not listed here.

## Limits that apply
- Singers mustn't need a password: a singer must be able to tell us they will miss a rehearsal without one (this bears on tell-absence; section leaders already use the hall's computer, which has no accounts).
- The register is on a small computer at the church hall that the section leaders use: what singers tell us has to end up in that register, since that is where we look.

## Not yet confirmed
- **Reaching the hall's computer, bearing on tell-absence:** whether singers can reach the hall's computer from home. We think not, but we're not sure and haven't thought about it; we don't know yet. This is not asked again until we do.
- **From where, bearing on both items:** the register holds only the dates people came, not a list of rehearsals. Is every Thursday a rehearsal, or are some Thursdays off? The answer changes which rehearsals a singer can say they will miss.
- **When, bearing on record-phoned-absence:** does the Tuesday-evening limit apply to a section leader recording a phoned absence too? And what happens to a notice that comes after Tuesday evening: is it turned away, or taken anyway?
- **Which, bearing on both items:** does a singer tell us about one rehearsal at a time, or can they cover several at once (for example a holiday)? The answer may split the item in two.
- **Put right, bearing on both items:** if a singer finds they can come after all, or told us the wrong date, can they take the notice back? The answer would add an item.
- **Does, bearing on both items:** do we want a reason with the notice, or only the fact that they will be away? The answer changes what the singer is asked.

## Outside systems and lasting things
- The register at the church hall (who is in the choir, in which voice part, and who came to which rehearsal): exists today, kept on the hall's computer.
- A rehearsal: every Thursday, as far as the documents say.
- A singer's notice that they will miss a rehearsal: new; it is what lets us know in advance who will be away.
