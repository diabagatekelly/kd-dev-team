# Slices: A singer tells us in advance that they'll miss a rehearsal

| # | Slice | Items | Builds on | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A section leader records that a singer who phoned will miss a rehearsal | record-for-singer | nothing | chosen |
| 2 | A singer tells us by Tuesday evening that they'll miss Thursday's rehearsal | tell-absence-ahead | 1 | open |
| 3 | A section leader is stopped from recording an absence after the Tuesday evening cut-off | record-for-singer | 1 | open |

## Where to start
- Slice 1 can be started now. I recommend it first: it works at the hall's computer where the register already is, needs no password, and doesn't lean on any open question. It also gives absences a place to be kept, which slice 2 needs.
- Slice 2 cannot be started until we know whether singers can reach the hall's computer from home. That decides where a singer can tell us.
- Slice 3 cannot be started until we know whether the Tuesday evening cut-off also applies when a section leader records an absence.
