## A singer's word that they will miss a rehearsal

In this slice this is the absence a section leader records.

| State | Entered by | What the person can do | Way out | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `recorded` | a section leader records it for a singer who has phoned them | the person who plans the programme sees the singer's name, counted under their voice part, for that rehearsal only | none | slice-001/004_slice.md lines 9, 12 and 13 |
| `refused` | the name is not in the register, or the rehearsal has passed; no absence is recorded | the section leader is told which of the two it is; what they can do if it is refused every time is not said | none needed: nothing is kept | slice-001/004_slice.md lines 10 and 11 |
| `sent-by-singer` | the singer lets the choir know themselves (slice 2); whether it then differs from `recorded` is not said | the person who plans the programme sees it | none | 002_requirements.md lines 3 and 5 |
| `wrong-or-withdrawn` | a mistake in recording, or the singer finding they can come after all | a section leader can tell the person who plans the programme that the absence shown is wrong; it stays shown | none | 002_requirements.md line 22; slice-001/004_slice.md line 26 |
| `rehearsal-passed` | the date of its rehearsal passes | not said whether it is kept, shown, or counts as word for working out who has gone quiet | none | not said in the documents |

## For the owner's list
- `in-register`: no way out. A singer stays in the register, and the documents do not say how one is taken out.
- `not-in-register`: no way out. A section leader cannot record an absence under that name, and the documents do not say what they do next.
- `asked-if-coming`: no way out. The documents do not say what follows either answer, or no answer.
- `changed-voice-part`: no way out. The documents do not say how a singer's part is changed, so absences could be counted under the old part.
- `left-the-choir`: no way out. The documents do not say what happens to the singer or to absences already recorded for them.
- `passed`: no way out. The documents do not say how long a past rehearsal is kept.
- `cancelled-or-moved`: no way out. The documents do not say what happens to absences recorded for that date.
- `recorded`: no way out. The documents give no way to change or remove it.
- `sent-by-singer`: no way out. The documents give no way for the singer to change or take back their word.
- `wrong-or-withdrawn`: no way out. The singer is still shown and counted as going to miss the rehearsal, so their voice part can look shorter than it is.
- `rehearsal-passed`: no way out. The documents do not say what becomes of the absence.
