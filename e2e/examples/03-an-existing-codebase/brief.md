# A new need in a choir's attendance register

An application that already exists and is used, and one more thing its users need: the choir's
conductor, who uses the register every week, did not write it and cannot read code.

## Opening
We already have this little attendance register for the choir, it's in this folder. What I need now is for singers to be able to tell us in advance when they're going to miss a rehearsal.

planning

## Answers
| Key | When the assistant asks about | The user says |
|---|---|---|
| folder | where the planning documents are or should go | Put them in a folder called planning. |
| today | how absences are handled today, what the choir does now | We're about 45 singers in four voice parts and we rehearse every Thursday. A singer who'll miss one texts me, or tells a friend, or says nothing. |
| wrong | what goes wrong, what it costs | I find out on the night that the tenors are three short, and by then I can't rearrange the programme. |
| anything-else | whether anything else goes wrong, whether that is everything | One more thing. A singer who misses three rehearsals in a row without a word is asked whether they're still coming. The secretary works that out by hand today. |
| why-now | why now, the deadline | The spring concert is in ten weeks, and two of the pieces can't be sung with fewer than four tenors. |
| limits | hard limits: rules, who can use what, where it runs | Singers mustn't need a password, several are in their eighties. And the register is on a small computer at the church hall that the section leaders use. |
